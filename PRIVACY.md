# Privacy Policy — My To-Do : Daily Planner

Last updated: [publish date]

My To-Do : Daily Planner ("the app") stores your tasks, calendar, diary, attachments and settings only on your PC.

## Version 1.0.0.0

- **No data collection.** The developer collects no personal data, analytics, telemetry or crash reports, and shows no ads.
- **No network connections.** This version does not connect to the internet. Nothing you enter leaves your PC.
- **Local storage.** Your data is kept in the app's own folder in your Windows user profile, protected by your Windows account permissions. A local log file is written only for troubleshooting and never sent anywhere.
- **Your control.** You can export all your data to JSON or your calendar to an .ics file at any time from Settings. Uninstalling the app removes its data from your PC.

## Version 1.1.0.0 and later (optional Google sign-in)

- **Optional.** Signing in with Google is never required; without it the app works fully and nothing leaves your PC.
- **What the app accesses.** If you choose to sign in, the app uses your Google account only to read your email address (to show which account is connected) and to store a copy of your app data in a hidden, app-only folder in your own Google Drive. The app cannot see any other files in your Drive, your Gmail or your Google Calendar.
- **Where data goes.** Synced data travels directly between your PC and Google over an encrypted connection. It is never sent to the developer or any other party.
- **Local backups.** Backups are written to a folder you choose on your PC and stay there.
- **Your control.** You can sign out at any time; signing out keeps your data on the PC. You can delete the cloud copy from the app's Settings, or remove the app's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
- **Google API use.** The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Children

The app is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children.
